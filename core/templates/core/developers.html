Search stock

GET /api/v1/search/?q=<medicine>[&district=Gasabo][&lat=-1.95&lng=30.10]

Verified pharmacies holding the medicine (brand or generic name). Typos are corrected automatically and reported in corrected_query. With lat/lng, results include distance_km and are sorted nearest first.

List medicines
