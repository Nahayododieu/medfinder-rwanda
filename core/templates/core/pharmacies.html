Questions from pharmacies

Who can join?

Any pharmacy in Rwanda with a valid Rwanda FDA license. We verify your license before patients can see you.

How long does set-up take?

About 20 minutes. Register online, then add your medicines one by one or upload an Excel sheet. We can help you on site.

Do I need a computer?

No. The dashboard works on a phone, and you can update stock by SMS: STOCK Paracetamol 500mg 40 120.

What about prescription-only medicines?

They can be reserved, but they are marked, and you check the prescription at the counter as usual.

Who sees my data?

Patients see your name, location, phone, hours, medicines in stock and prices. Your sales figures are never shown to other pharmacies.
