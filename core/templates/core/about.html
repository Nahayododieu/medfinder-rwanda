Our mission

Make every medicine in Rwanda easy to find: on a smartphone, on a basic phone by USSD, in Kinyarwanda, English or French.

The problem we solve

Today a patient with a prescription often visits or phones several pharmacies before finding the medicine, losing time and transport money and sometimes delaying treatment. Pharmacies, meanwhile, cannot see the demand they are missing.

How we work

  • Verified pharmacies only. We check every pharmacy's Rwanda FDA license before it appears.
  • Live stock and prices. Pharmacies update stock from a dashboard, Excel, SMS or USSD.
  • Reserve and collect. Patients reserve with a phone number and collect with an SMS code; payment at the counter or by mobile money.
  • Privacy first. Patients give only a phone number. Demand statistics are anonymous.
  • No medical advice. We inform; pharmacists advise and dispense.
