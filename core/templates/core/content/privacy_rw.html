Amakuru dukusanya

  • Abarwayi: nimero ya telefoni wanditse gusa iyo ubikishije cyangwa wishyura, umuti n’ingano wabikishije. Ntitukubaza izina ryawe, indwara cyangwa indangamuntu.
  • Ishakisha: izina ry’umuti washakishije, akarere, n’uko wabonetse cyangwa utabonetse. Ishakisha ribikwa nta nimero ya telefoni cyangwa aho uri bigaragaramo.
  • Aho uri: niba ubyemeye, aho mushakisha yawe iri bikoreshwa gusa mu gutondeka farumasi zikwegereye muri iryo shakisha. Ntibibikwa.
  • Farumasi: izina ry’ubucuruzi, nimero y’uruhushya, telefoni, aderesi n’aho iri ku ikarita, hamwe n’ububiko n’ibiciro zitangaza.

Impamvu tuyakoresha

  • Kukoherereza kode yawe n’amakuru ku miti wabikishije ukoresheje SMS.
  • Kwemerera farumasi kwemeza umuti wabikishije, kandi, niba ubihisemo, gukora kwishyura kuri MTN Mobile Money.
  • Gukora imibare itagaragaza abantu (urugero, imiti ikunze kubura mu karere) ifasha farumasi, abatanga imiti n’abategura iby’ubuzima kugabanya ibura ry’imiti.

Abashobora kuyabona

Farumasi wabikishijeho ni yo yonyine ibona nimero yawe ya telefoni. Abayobozi bashobora kureba amakuru kugira ngo bagenzure farumasi kandi bakemure ibibazo. Imibare rusange itagaragaza abantu ishobora gusangizwa abafatanyabikorwa; ntijya igira nimero za telefoni.

Abatanga serivisi

SMS na USSD bitangwa binyuze kuri Africa’s Talking, kwishyura binyuze kuri MTN Mobile Money, naho amakarita akoresha OpenStreetMap. Aba batanga serivisi bahabwa gusa ibyo bakeneye kugira ngo batange serivisi yabo.
